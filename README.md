
# 🚗 Alt Mobility – Data Analyst Assignment

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of customer, order, and payment data for **Alt Mobility**, focused on understanding sales performance, customer behavior, payment outcomes, and retention patterns. The project was executed using **MySQL** for querying and **Power BI** for data visualization.


## 🧠 Objectives

- Analyze order trends and revenue flow over time
- Segment customers by value and activity
- Understand payment success and failure patterns
- Build a consolidated order-payment report
- Perform **cohort-based retention analysis** with visual insights

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **SQL (MySQL)** – for data extraction and transformation
- **Power BI** – for dashboard and data visualization
- **Excel** (optional) – for pivot and cohort shaping

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### ✅ Sales & Orders
- Total orders: 2,880 | Total revenue: ₹1M+
- Revenue peaked in 2021 and showed a steady decline post that.

### ✅ Customers
- 37% of customers are repeat buyers
- Most fall into low/medium-value spend brackets
- Clear opportunity to improve engagement and upselling

### ✅ Payments
- 85% of payments are successful
- Failed payments spike during first-purchase periods

### ✅ Retention
- Retention drops from 100% → 2–5% by month 1
- Most cohorts churn by month 2–3
- Strong case for post-purchase engagement & loyalty programs

---

## 📈 Visual Highlights

- 📉 **Monthly Customer Acquisition Trend** – declining steadily from 2020 to 2025
- 🧊 **Customer Retention Heatmap** – reveals sharp early churn

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Implement loyalty and retention programs
- Fix friction in the payment process (especially for first-time users)
- Focus on retaining and growing high-value customers
- Analyze drop-off points after first order
